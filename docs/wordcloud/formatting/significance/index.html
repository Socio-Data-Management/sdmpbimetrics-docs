Significance

The Significance card decides which words are significantly more or less frequent than a reference, and how they are highlighted.

Properties​

PropertyDescriptionDefault
TestNone, Vs global average or Vs comparison measure — see belowNone
Confidence level90%, 95% or 99%95%
VariancePooled or Separate — see VarianceSeparate
Minimum baseWords whose Base is below this are never flagged30
Above referenceColor of a word significantly above the reference#cc0000
Below referenceColor of a word significantly below the reference#007700
Not significantColor of every other word#888888
Highlight styleColor only, Color + Bold or Color + Outline (a significant word only)Color + Bold

The two tests​

Both are a two-proportion z-test: z = (p1 − p2) / √variance, where p1 = Value / Base for the word.

Vs global average​

The reference is the rest of the cloud: p2 = (total Value − word Value) / (total Base − word Base). The word is left out of its own reference, so it is never compared against a total that contains itself. The totals are computed on all the words that remain after cleaning, before Max words is applied. Needs Base.

Vs comparison measure​

The reference is the word's own p2 = Comparison / Comparison Base: last period, another segment, a benchmark. Needs Base, Comparison and Comparison Base.

In both modes the word stays Not significant when its Base is below Minimum base or when the reference base is zero.

Variance​

The two methods give close results when the bases are similar and the proportions not extreme.

MethodVarianceReads as
Separate (default)p1(1−p1)/n1 + p2(1−p2)/n2Each proportion keeps its own variance
Pooledp(1−p)(1/n1 + 1/n2), with p the pooled proportionVariance under the hypothesis that both proportions are equal

Colors while a test is on​

As soon as Test is not None, every word takes one of the three significance colors, whatever the Colors card says. Only a Series Color measure can still override them.
