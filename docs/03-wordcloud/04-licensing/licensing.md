# Licensing

## Editions

### Free

- All features
- **"Powered by Socio DM" watermark** displayed on the visual
- Community support

### Pro

- Everything in Free
- **No watermark**
- Email support
- Licensed per organization

## Purchasing from Microsoft AppSource

To purchase a Pro license, search for **"SDM WordCloud"** on the [Microsoft AppSource](https://appsource.microsoft.com) marketplace and click **Get it now**. Once you have a valid license, Power BI automatically detects it and unlocks the Pro features.

## Entering a License Key

You can also buy directly from Socio-DM, especially for volume licensing, or receive a key from your organization.

### Directly for one visual

1. Select the visual in your report
2. Open the **Format** pane
3. Expand the **License** card
4. Paste your license key into the **License Key** field

### For bulk activation across multiple visuals or an entire organization

A volume or site license comes with a Theme (.json) file. Applying it to a report unlocks the Pro features on all the purchased visuals, without entering individual keys.

The license is validated automatically. The watermark disappears once a valid Pro license is detected.

## License Information

Click the small **(i)** icon at the top right corner of the visual. The About dialog shows the name, the version, the plan (Free or Pro) and, for Pro, the organization and the expiry date.

## Microsoft Fabric Licensing

When the visual is deployed in a Fabric workspace with an active SDM subscription, the Pro plan is detected automatically.

License resolution priority:

1. **Microsoft service plan** — Fabric subscription (auto-detected)
2. **License Key** — value typed in the License card
3. **Free** — fallback with watermark
